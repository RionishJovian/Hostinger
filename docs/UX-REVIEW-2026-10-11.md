# MikroMagic interface review — 2026-10-11 (P0a, Hybrid UX)

Owner directive (Notion roadmap P0a): the current wording, terminology, flows and screens feel messy. Direction **Hybrid**: one product, simple guided screens by default, advanced controls and technical detail available on demand; every role; phone-first readability; no loss of existing functions or safety confirmations.

## Method

- Throwaway local database seeded through the real API: one SME owner (business, shop, two Internet packages, four vouchers, one dealer), the platform owner (Developer), one approved Agent. Nothing touched production.
- Every navigation page per role captured in Chrome at desktop (1366 px) and phone (390 px) widths — 72 captures — with automatic measures: words, visible buttons and inputs, tables, panels, page height, horizontal overflow, squeezed table cells, and the elements that overflow on phones.
- Source scan of competing terms.
- Not yet covered: the guest portal (needs a published shop link), the Burmese wording quality itself, and task walkthroughs with real users.

## Owner terminology decisions (2026-10-12 00:10)

Shop (not Site), Internet Pass / Media Pass with a "Passes" catalogue page (not Package), Router (Gateway only in technical details). Voucher states Generated / Sold / Activated stay distinct.

## Plan — small deployed increments

1. **Page frame (F1–F3, F11):** phone header fits (Refresh and Sign out become labelled icon buttons on narrow screens); tables scroll inside their box on phones; remove developer-facing footer, subtitle, sidebar and eyebrow text for signed-in users (the demo keeps its sample-data note); portal page tolerates older drafts.
2. **SME navigation (F4):** group the menu into a few labelled sections (daily work first, setup and technical pages later) without removing pages.
3. **Vouchers (F5):** "Issue a voucher" first; inventory, printing and batches, dealers and stock search as separate tabs.
4. **Overview (F7):** one health message, then Needs your attention, Quick Voucher and the daily summary; revenue detail stays on Revenue.
5. **Routers (F6):** guided "Connect your router" steps by default; Magic Hub plans, keys and RouterOS changes under an Advanced (technician) section.
6. **Terminology and Burmese (F8–F9):** apply the owner's chosen terms everywhere and complete the Burmese strings for SME screens.
7. **Developer Dashboard (F10)** and the Agents table on phones; then the guest portal review and task walkthroughs (sign in, first shop, sell a voucher, renew, approve an Agent, buy a Media Pass).

## Progress

- **Increment 1 — page frame (2026-10-11):** done. Phone header fits (Refresh and Sign out are labelled icon buttons below 720 px); table cells keep a readable minimum width and scroll inside their box; the developer footer, the "account database · Your authorized workspace" subtitle, the sidebar "Authenticated records"/adapters note and the eyebrow are gone for signed-in users (the demo keeps its sample-data note and footer); the Captive portal page tolerates drafts without `allowed_hosts`/`payment_methods`. Re-measured at 390 px: 0 of 32 owner, Developer and Agent pages scroll sideways (was 18 of 18 owner pages).
- **Increment 2 — SME menu (2026-10-12):** done. The 18 pages are grouped under Sell (Vouchers, Payments, Revenue, Passes, Media), Shops & network (Shops, Routers, Network, Internet, Guest Wi-Fi page), Business (Subscription, Connected services, Activity history, Account) and Help (Get help, Did you know?), after Overview and Notifications; headings in English and Burmese. Menu labels use the owner's terms (Shops, Passes) and plainer names (Guest Wi-Fi page, Connected services, Activity history). The menu scrolls inside the sidebar on short windows (F12). Page contents still use the old words until the terminology increment.
- **Increment 3 — Vouchers task-first (2026-10-12):** done. The page opens on **Issue a voucher**; All vouchers (metrics, inventory, sale review), Find stock, Batches & printing (batch sheets and production jobs) and Dealers are tabs (Dealers and production jobs only for the owner/admin, outside the demo). Inactive tabs stay mounted but hidden, so a code shown once survives switching tabs. A batch QR link opens Batches & printing. Labels in English and Burmese. Issue tab ≈970 px on desktop instead of the ≈4,900 px page; no sideways scrolling on a 390 px phone in any tab.
- **Increment 4 — Overview (2026-10-12):** done. The router state appears once, in the Wi-Fi health card, with its action (Open Routers, or Open Shops when no shop exists) next to Get help; Needs your attention lists only other items (maintenance, payment reviews) and otherwise says nothing else needs attention. Order: health, attention, Quick Voucher, daily summary, network summary. The three revenue figures, Revenue by service (with the retired "combined" row) and Business actions were removed; a Net confirmed collections card in the network summary opens Revenue. The daily summary's first row now fits its four figures. Desktop page ≈1,950 px (was ≈2,900); no sideways scrolling on a 390 px phone in English or Burmese.
- **Increment 5 — Routers guided + Advanced (2026-10-12):** done. The owner's Routers page opens on **Connect your router**: four steps (add your shop, register your router, connect it with a technician, check the connection), each marked Done, Next or Later, with one button on the next step. A router counts as connected only when its connection state says so (always unverified in this release). Then the register form, the router list and technician pairing. Magic Hub, connection plans (WireGuard keys, tunnel addresses) and Safe RouterOS changes are in a closed **Advanced (technician)** section that opens by itself when a router change needs attention (conflict, recovery, failure, rollback). Platform and demo views unchanged. Guide in English and Burmese; the sections below it are translated in the terminology step. Desktop ≈1,800 px with Advanced closed; no sideways scrolling on a 390 px phone.
